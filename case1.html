Business Gamification Platform

2023

UX/UI Design for Workplace Gamification Software and Employee Performance Management.

Overview

The challenge

The product was designed as a business gamification platform helping companies engage employees, organise activities and monitor performance.

My role focused on UX/UI design for the software interface, with an emphasis on making complex information easier to understand and giving users clear ways to interact with the platform.

Interface design

The interface combined dashboards, employee activities, performance information and gamification mechanics within one software environment.

The design process included structuring information, developing interface layouts and creating reusable UI patterns for different parts of the product.

Design approach

The main focus was to balance the amount of information available to users with a clear and understandable interface structure.

Different user scenarios were considered when organising navigation, content hierarchy and interaction patterns across the platform.

Result

The resulting interface provided a structured environment for workplace gamification and employee performance management.

The project involved designing a consistent experience across different areas of the product while keeping the interface practical for everyday business use.
